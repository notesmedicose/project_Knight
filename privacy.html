Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Knight 3D Chess ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. No Data Collection

Knight 3D Chess is a fully offline game. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account, sign-up, or login is required.

2. No Internet Required

The core game runs entirely offline on your device. No internet connection is needed to play.

3. AdMob Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous usage data to serve relevant ads. This data may include device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID), general location (not precise GPS), and app usage patterns. You can reset or limit the Advertising ID in your device settings.

For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13. The ads served are standard banner ads that do not involve tracking for children under the applicable age of consent.

5. Data Storage

Game state preferences (like sound settings) are stored locally on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Google AdMob for banner ads. No other analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are used.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through our app's support page on the Google Play Store.
